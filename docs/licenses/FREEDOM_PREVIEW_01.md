# Freedom preview 01 — attribution and provenance

The preview combines code-authored Apsis Drift geometry, Godot viewport imagery,
and an offline render of the existing First Light score.

- Visual geometry, shaders, camera plan and titles: Apsis Drift contributors,
  [BSD-3-Clause](../../LICENSE.md).
- First Light MIDI composition and scheduling: Apsis Drift contributors,
  BSD-3-Clause; rendered by the existing C++/TinySoundFont audio path.
- First Light SoundFont ambient source: generated with Stable Audio 2.5 through
  Venice.ai, with code-authored supporting instruments. The retained
  [First Light generated-output terms](FIRST_LIGHT_GENERATED_OUTPUT.md) and the
  exact parent records in `assets/provenance.json` apply. This film does not
  replace or broaden that upstream provenance assessment.
- Rendered in Godot Engine 4.7.2, then encoded with FFmpeg. No engine code is
  embedded in the video. It is an SDR rendering preview, not HDR or gameplay.

The combined video records
`BSD-3-Clause AND LicenseRef-Apsis-First-Light-Generated-Output` in the manifest.
Retain these credits and the referenced terms when redistributing it. No new
third-party imagery, voices or music were downloaded for this preview.
