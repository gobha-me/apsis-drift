# Charted planetary trips

Choose **Four systems + planetary charts** under **Starting charts** for a new
journey. C++ generates the same four systems and their ordinary planets from
the universe seed. The chart grants names and orbital locations. Physical
properties, surface findings and visits remain separate evidence acquired by
the existing observation, survey and landing actions.

The other two starting-chart choices retain their existing behavior. Continue
keeps the saved selection; reading a chart never upgrades an old journey.

## Make a trip

1. Board, depart and reach suitable space flight. Pause with neutral controls.
2. In Controls, choose **Select planet** for another charted planet in the
   current system. **Current reference** identifies the body used for flight;
   **visited** comes independently from recorded landing presence. Arriving in
   space around a planet does not claim a surface visit.
3. Choose **Begin selected jump**, then Resume explicitly. The ordinary
   three-second spool runs physics and flight-fuel accounting. Canceling the
   spool spends no jump charge. Commitment spends one charge; the following
   two-second transit advances the same authoritative clock.
4. At arrival, fly and descend manually. Deploy gear, land on suitable terrain,
   walk, inspect or collect, reboard and ascend through the existing controls.
5. Select a charted return destination and budget another charge. Physical
   docking at Origin remains the source of free fuel and jump replenishment.

Arrival uses the planet's actual future orbit and rotation. Its complete hull
is placed at least 100 km beyond the greater of the atmosphere boundary and conservative
terrain envelope. Endpoint checks include the star, planets and secondary
bodies. The jump retains source-relative inertial velocity, attitude and angular
motion; an orbit-hold request for the former body is cleared. Keep your arrival
speed in mind. A clear arrival is not a qualified landing or ascent guarantee.

Origin's station appears only at its actual host planet. Other planets have no
invented ports or free service. Three charges still bound a trip: a planetary
out-and-back consumes two. Route selection, reading and Continue spend nothing.

## Ownership and persistence

Knowledge recipe5/chart3 explicitly selects these starting grants. The existing
observation policy and physical generators keep their meanings. Destination
rows require resolved identity and location records before catalog lookup;
Godot does not enumerate hidden bodies or calculate arrivals.

The first selected route in this new journey selects travel format56/state5.
One shared phase, clock and attempt counter owns system, planet, Moon and minor
trips. Fixed typed receipt slots retain each provider's latest commitment and
bill; companions also retain the actual current excursion's outbound model.
Returning to Origin's home planet permits the existing companion trips again.
Ordinary planetary and system trips can follow each other without erasing
those receipts. Direct transfers from companions still return home first.

Craft, pilot, flight fuel, observations, surveys, personal metadata, specimens
and field notes follow the same voyage. Save/Continue preserves spool,
commitment, transit and arrival. Standard replacement retains chart grants and
notes, losing carried specimens under the existing recovery policy.

Historical charts and travel formats retain their validators and bytes. This
increment does not opt existing journeys into a new chart, add route planning,
change jump-risk rules or introduce missions and paid services.
