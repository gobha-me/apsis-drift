
## All-caps complement and equality

Every capsule point has `p−H=s*u+q`, `0<=s<=L1`, `||q||<=r`. Define `t=u·(p−H)` and `v=p−H−t*u`. Genuine unit authority gives `v⊥u` and `||v||<=r`, including root hemisphere, cylinder and distal hemisphere. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds

```text
vY <= r*sqrt(uX²+uZ²).
E = L*uY + r*sqrt(uX²+uZ²).
```

For an outside-slab point `t>L` with `uY<0`, multiplication reverses the strict inequality:

```text
pY−HY = t*uY+vY < E.
```

Consequently exact `E<=Q`, including **E=Q**, puts every outside-slab capsule point strictly below the pelvis and proves containment. This exact implication differs from the implemented sufficient test: [self02_hip_expression](../src/origin_boarding_planted_legs.cpp), lines 4415–4433, and ep4_hip, lines 8396–8440, require `uY.upper<0` and **`E.upper<Q.lower` strictly**. Equality or straddling refuses that selected predicate. This audit proposes no relaxation.

The root cap has `t<=r<=L`, hence cannot escape the slab. The unified perpendicular-radius bound also covers the distal cap; it introduces no shortened-capsule distal hemisphere. Supported squares/add/value-root/products/sum, ordered finite operands and authentic constants remain mandatory. A zero transverse value-root is permitted, without granting derivative authority. `uY=0` or positive is outside this negative-axis argument.
